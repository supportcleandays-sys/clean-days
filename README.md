# clean-days
A free recovery app for counting clean time including other helpful features.

Clean Days Privacy Policy
Effective date: August 31, 2026
Clean Days is designed to be a private, local-first recovery support app. The app does not require an account and does not operate a cloud database containing your personal recovery information.
Information stored on your device
Clean Days may store information locally on your device, including your clean date and time, display preferences, custom counters, gratitude entries, affirmation favorites, personal affirmations, reminder preferences, favorite meetings, and other app settings.
This information is intended to remain on your device and is not uploaded to a Clean Days server.
Accounts and analytics
Clean Days does not require you to create an account.
Clean Days does not use advertising trackers or behavioral analytics to monitor how you use the app.
Location and Meeting Finder
If you choose to use the Meeting Finder, Clean Days may request access to your device location so it can search for nearby Narcotics Anonymous meetings.
Location information used for meeting searches may be sent to third-party services necessary to provide meeting and map functionality. These may include BMLT meeting services and map or geocoding providers.
Clean Days does not use your location to track your movements or build a location history.
You may also search for meetings by entering a city or address instead of using your current location.
Notifications
Clean Days may schedule local notifications on your device for features such as clean-time reminders, gratitude reminders, and affirmation reminders.
Notification preferences are stored locally on your device. Clean Days does not use a remote notification server to send these reminders.
External services and links
Some features open or communicate with third-party services, including:
- BMLT or related services for meeting information
- Map and geocoding services for meeting locations and directions
- Official recovery-reading websites and external reading links
- Venmo or another external service if you choose to use the optional Donate feature
When you use those services, information may be processed according to the privacy policies of those third parties.
Data sharing
Clean Days does not sell your personal information.
Clean Days does not operate a system for sharing your personal recovery information with advertisers, data brokers, or other users.
Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when necessary to perform a feature you choose to use, such as searching for nearby meetings or opening an external link.
Data retention and deletion
Information stored by Clean Days remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app, subject to normal operating-system backup behavior.
Because Clean Days does not maintain a user account or cloud database containing your recovery data, there is generally no Clean Days server-side account or recovery history to delete.
Sensitive recovery information
Clean Days is intended to minimize collection of sensitive information. Your clean date, gratitude entries, personal affirmations, and similar recovery-related information are intended to remain locally stored on your device.
Children
Clean Days is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly operate a system for collecting personal information from children.
Security
Clean Days is designed to minimize privacy risk by avoiding unnecessary collection and centralized storage of personal recovery information. No software or device storage system can guarantee absolute security.
Changes to this Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as Clean Days changes. If the app begins collecting or processing information in a materially different way, this policy will be updated accordingly.
